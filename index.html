Pocket Looper

A minimal five-track loop station in the browser. Record a loop, then layer four more tracks in sync.

This preview is embedded in a frame that blocks the microphone. Open the looper in its own browser tab to record.

Wear headphones to avoid feedback. Your audio never leaves this device: no upload, no server.

No loop yet

Input
  1. Press track 1, make a sound, press it again. That sets the loop length and it starts playing.
  2. Press any other track: it waits for the next loop, records exactly one loop, then plays in sync.
  3. Tap a playing track to stop it, tap again to bring it back in sync. Volume slides, Clear wipes one track.
  4. Clear every track to reset the loop length and start over.

Free, no account, no upload. Everything runs in this browser tab.

Recording uses your browser's Web Audio API. Left out to stay minimal: overdub, effects, undo and saving loops, so timing can sit a few milliseconds off a hardware unit.
